Time To Reboot

Posted: 02 May 2026
Currently reading: Nothing really
Currently binging: Star Trek Enterprise, sort of but not really
Current music genre: Nothing really

So last night I grabbed the laptop and booted the Linux side. Then I opened Emacs and inside of Emacs I opened the RSS feedreader Elfeed. Then I spent about an hour reading posts from the four friends that alone comprise my entire feed.

LOVED. EVERY. MINUTE.

I was transported to Italy as I read posts of one friend walking parts of a loop around forty-four small churches somewhere near is home town. Another friend told tales about pouring a cup of coffee into his laptop. Yet another regaled me with stories of living in Israel in a time of war. It was a joy hearing their tales and seeing their pictures.

This is the way the internet, or at least socializing online, should be. Simple people telling us about their lives with simple tech. All of these guys follow each other via RSS and they only way to respond to most of them is via email. Both are very simple tech.

So the long and short of it is that I got the itch to be a part of this small web revolution again and this is the first post of my reboot.

I have a few decisions to make as to "how" I am going to do this but I am back out in the world living the small web life.
