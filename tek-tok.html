Ok, here is a piece for the geeky, techie crowd. I don't really want to have many "geeky" articles here. I am not really a geek, per se. My day job is as a professional cabinetmaker. Computers and ham radios have always been more of a hobby than an avocation. But I do try to be geeky from time to time.

The Hardware

My tools are:

  • A 15" Lenovo Ideapad 3
  • Some sort of Lenovo tablet which hardly gets used
  • A Google Pixel 10 Pro phone

On the Ideapad I have the Windows Subsystem for Linux working and the subsystem is mostly all about Emacs. I am no expert on Emacs. Off and on for about 20 years now I play with Emacs. I'll play with it until something makes me mad or confuses me and then I hang it up for a while. Then I'll start the cycle all over again.

The daily driver side of the laptop is whatever the latest iteration of Windows is. Not a fan of Windows but it is what I need for work purposes.

How this blog is written

Simply. ;-)

I have templates for a webpage and for the RSS item. I open VSCode and "fill in the blanks" and then push everything to Github.

Dreams

I always have the dream of doing everything through Emacs but to tell the truth that may not ever really happen. Mostly this may not happen because I tend to be on the flaky side, but also...

...I wonder why I have so much computer stuff and am tempted to ditch everything but the phone and make that my sole device for everything. Truth is that if Android had a computer-aided drafting app that was worth half-a-damn I would've done it already. I need a drafting program for work.

Over the last few days I have discovered that even with a mouse and external keyboard blogging via the phone might be an issue too. So, you know, I might just keep doing what I am doing for a little while longer.

So there you have it. It ain't much but there it is.
