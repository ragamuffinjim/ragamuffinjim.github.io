A New Look

And it’s, hopefully, lighter weight too.

It fits some key design criteria

Let me describe the website:

  • It's lightweight and loads fast.
  • Fits on all your screens.
  • Looks the same in all browsers.
  • Accessible to every one that visits your site.
  • It's legible and gets the point across.
  • Hopefully, it is lightweight enough for our friends with limited internet.
  • It contains NO JavaScript.
  • It makes no internet calls for things like fonts or css files etc.

All in all, so far, I am liking this look. It has the added feature of simplicity. It also conforms, mostly (this is a quickie page), Brutalist Web Design, which idea I am generally in favour of.
